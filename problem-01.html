About the college

Situated in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, GLA University is a top-tier UGC-approved and NAAC A+ accredited campus known for academic excellence. The institution focuses heavily on experiential learning through strategic partnerships with global tech giants like Microsoft and Google AI. With an impressive placement record, an average package of ₹8.1 LPA, and state-of-the-art infrastructure, GLA University Greater Noida is the perfect launchpad for students aiming to excel in the global corporate landscape.

Why I like my college?
