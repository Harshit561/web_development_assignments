National Career & Education Fair

Join the National Career & Education Fair to explore career options, education programs, internships, scholarships and placement opportunities.

The event brings students, job seekers, companies, universities and career experts together. It helps participants understand different career paths and make better career decisions.

“Your career journey starts with the right opportunity.”

Preparation Guide

Preparation Tips

  • Research companies before attending.
  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Carry your college ID card.
  • Wear professional clothes.
  • Prepare a short introduction.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Research organizations.
  3. Attend useful sessions.
  4. Meet recruiters.
  5. Apply for opportunities.
  6. Follow up after the event.
Notice: Job seekers should carry multiple copies of their resume.

Information and FAQs

What is this event?
It is a career and education fair for students and job seekers.
Is registration free?
Yes, general registration is free.
Who can attend?
Students, graduates and job seekers can attend.
Should I bring a resume?
Yes, job seekers should bring an updated resume.
Are internships available?
Yes, companies will provide internship information.
