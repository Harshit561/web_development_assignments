Event Overview

Welcome to the National Career & Education Fair 2026, India's premier platform connecting ambitious students, recent graduates, and seasoned professionals with leading global enterprises and top-tier academic institutions. This year's mega event focuses on driving career transformation, fostering industry-academia collaboration, and uncovering unprecedented employment horizons. Whether you are looking for your first job or planning higher education, this fair is your definitive stepping stone to success.

Event Objectives

Our core mission centers on bridging the employment gap by offering direct recruitment gateways. We aim to provide comprehensive academic counseling for higher studies, deliver interactive skill-enhancement workshops, and facilitate exclusive networking opportunities with industry stalwarts and tech innovators.

"Empowering the next generation of professionals with the right opportunities at the right time is the cornerstone of sustainable national growth. We are thrilled to host this transformative confluence of talent and industry."

— Organizing Committee Chairperson
