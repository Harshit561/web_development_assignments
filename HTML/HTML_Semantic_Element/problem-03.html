Consultation Category Definitions

Consultation
The first detailed session where new health complaints are diagnosed and primary checkups are completed.

Follow-up
Subsequent shorter visits intended solely to review previous reports or track medication recovery progress.

OPD
Out-Patient Department services meant for general health checkups without requiring hospital ward admission.

Appointment
A pre-booked specific time slot reserved for meeting designated specialist doctors comfortably.
