Workshops & Preparation

Preparation Tips

  • Carry an updated resume.
  • Research the companies before attending.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Ask questions to company representatives.
  • Attend workshops according to your interests.
  • Keep your contact details ready.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the expo.
  2. Check the event schedule.
  3. Visit company stalls.
  4. Attend workshops and talks.
  5. Interact with speakers.
  6. Apply for suitable opportunities.
Tip: Carry your resume and student ID card while attending the expo.

Career Expo Terms

Career Expo
An event where students meet companies and explore career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary work experience that helps students gain practical knowledge.
Placement
A job opportunity offered to eligible students by a company.
Workshop
A practical session where participants learn about a particular topic.
Certification
A document showing that a person has completed a course or training.
Networking
Building professional connections with people from different industries.
