Learn Personal Finance

Smart Banking is an educational portal that helps students and customers understand banking, accounts, cards, loans and responsible personal-finance practices.

Good financial planning helps people manage their income, savings and expenses. It is important to understand interest rates, charges and repayment periods before choosing any financial product.

Important: Always read the terms and conditions before opening an account or taking a loan.

"Save today, plan tomorrow and spend responsibly." — Finance Educator

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is useful for keeping money safe and earning interest on savings. Customers can deposit and withdraw money according to account rules.

Current Account

A current account is mainly used by businesses and people who make frequent transactions.

Account Opening Steps

  1. Select the type of bank account.
  2. Fill in the application form.
  3. Submit identity and address documents.
  4. Complete verification.
  5. Make the required initial deposit.
  6. Receive account details from the bank.

Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows customers to spend money directly from their bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card allows customers to borrow money up to an approved limit and repay it later.

  • Keep your card and PIN safe.
  • Never share your OTP.
  • Check your transactions regularly.
  • Pay credit card bills on time.
  • Avoid unnecessary spending.

Loans and Finance

Personal Loan

A personal loan can be used for different personal expenses. The borrower has to repay the principal amount with interest.

Home Loan

A home loan helps customers finance the purchase or construction of a house.

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on the borrowed amount.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid by the borrower every month.
Principal
The original amount borrowed from the bank.
Tenure
The total period allowed for repayment of the loan.

Example: If principal = ₹5,00,000 and interest = 8% then the borrower must consider the interest cost before taking the loan.

Formula example: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time ÷ 100

Personal Finance Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save a part of your income.
  • Keep an emergency fund.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Pay bills before the due date.
  • Compare interest rates before borrowing.
  • Check your bank statements regularly.
  • Do not share banking passwords or OTPs.
Financial Awareness: Never share your OTP, PIN or internet banking password with anyone.
