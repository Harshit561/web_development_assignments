Student Terms & Rules



General Rules


All students must carry their digital ID cards at all times while on the campus premises. Sharing your student portal credentials with anyone is strictly prohibited. Note: This portal contains sensitive personal data and academic records.


Attendance


Regular classroom attendance is vital for your academic success and continuous evaluation. Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to sit for final semester examinations. Failure to meet this requirement will result in a mandatory grade drop penalty .


Examination


Final assessments are conducted under strict institutional supervision to ensure academic integrity. Bringing any unauthorized electronic devices or smartwatches into the exam hall is strictly banned. Any student caught using unfair means will face an immediate suspension for the entire semester .


Assignment Submission


Continuous assessments must be uploaded directly to the learning management platform. All final project submissions for the current term must be completed before Friday at 11:59 PM . Explanatory Note: No extension requests will be entertained after the portal automatically locks.


Discipline

The institution maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards any form of misconduct or bullying. Every student must respect the diversity and personal space of peers and faculty members. Any violation of code of conduct will lead to a permanent expulsion from the university .
