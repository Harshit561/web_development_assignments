Science Notes


Chemistry


Chemistry is the branch of science that studies the composition, structure, and properties of matter.

Formula 1:-H2Ois a universal solvent essential for all known forms of life.
Formula 2:-CO2 is a gas produced during respiration and used by plants in photosynthesis.
Formula 3:-O2 is crucial for the cellular respiration of aerobic organisms.
Formula 4:-H2SO4 is a highly corrosive mineral acid known as the king of chemicals.
Formula 5:-C6H12O6 is a simple sugar that serves as an primary energy source.

Physics


Physics is the natural science that studies matter, its fundamental constituents, and its motion through space and time.

Expression1 (Area measurement):-The standard SI unit for surface area is square meters, written as m2.

Expression2 (Volume measurement):-The space occupied by a three-dimensional object is measured in cubic meters, written as m3.
Expression3 (Speed of light):-In a vacuum, light travels at a constant velocity of approximately 3 × 108 meters per second, which is a fundamental universal constant.

Biology


Biology is the scientific study of life, encompassing the structure, function, growth, and evolution of living organisms.

Cell:-The Cell is defined as the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells that replicate independently.
