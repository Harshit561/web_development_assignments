TechFest 2026: Innovate, Integrate, Inspire



Introduction

Welcome to TechFest 2026, the ultimate annual destination for young innovators, tech enthusiasts, and engineering students across the country. Organized by the College Cultural and Technical Committee, this three-day extravaganza provides an unparalleled platform to showcase talent, learn from global industry pioneers, and collaborate on groundbreaking ideas that will shape the future of technology.


Highlights




We are absolutely thrilled to announce that this year features the country's largest National Hackathon with a grand cash prize of $5,000! Alongside the main coding battle, students can participate in intense Robo-Wars, drone racing simulators, open-source development marathons, and a massive dynamic gaming arena.


Date and Venue

Dates: October 12th to October 14th,


2026 Main Convention Centre, Auditorium Block,


Tech Campus New Delhi, India - 110001


Speakers Section


Our speaker lineup this year includes world-renowned artificial intelligence researchers, top cloud solution architects, and successful tech startup founders. Attendees will gain exclusive insights during the keynotes on quantum computing architectures, ethics in machine learning, and how to scale decentralized web applications in the modern ecosystem.


Registration Section


Registration is completely free for all active college identity card holders, but seats for individual workshops are strictly limited. Ensure you lock your slot before the final registration deadline on October 5, 2026 to get access to all event kits and food coupons.
